ACADEMY POLICY

Privacy Notice

Teacher Nelly's Young Learners Academy collects information submitted by parents or guardians for learner registration, inquiries, class access and payment-related support. We use this information to provide academy services and manage access.

Children's information

Parents or guardians should submit learner information. Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive information. The academy should use appropriate safeguards for children's information and comply with applicable privacy and child-protection requirements.

Payments

Payments are processed through Paystack. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by this website.

Advertising

The site may display Google AdSense advertising after approval. Advertising settings and availability are controlled by Google and must follow Google's publisher policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the academy through the parent inquiry form.
